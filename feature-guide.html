ISA Outcomes

Open a card to see the individual’s goal, their input, and how progress is measured.

For admins · Create an ISA card together

  1. Start with the individual. Look at a photo from My Month, a My STAR response, or an activity they remember. Ask what they liked, what they’d do again, or what they want to try. Give them time to answer using words, pictures, or choices. Their answer helps decide what goes on the card.
  2. Open ISA Outcomes and select + Add Outcome. Choose Create My Own Outcome or adapt a bank example together. Under What matters to the individual?, choose The individual’s own words for a direct quote. If they point to or select a picture, record what they chose and how they communicated it without putting words in their mouth. Label a caregiver or team observation separately as Observed preference.
  3. Write the goal together. Use their input and the approved ISA to shape the title and description. Agree on what support will help, who will provide it, and when. Under How Progress Is Documented, add what caregivers should record, such as minutes spent or steps completed.
  4. Read the card back together. Use words or pictures that work for the individual and give them a chance to correct it or come back later. Select Save Outcome Card as a draft if it still needs review. Once it’s ready, activate it and use Customize Caregiver Check-in to link an existing answer if it already measures the same thing.

Review progress and update the ISA

Quarterly check-ins. If your team reviews progress quarterly, use that time to ask the individual what’s working and look at their responses and the progress recorded in STAR. Vermont DDSD calls for a review of the full ISA at least annually and before a new ISA starts. Each outcome has its own review schedule written into the ISA. The Qualified Developmental Disabilities Professional (QDDP) follows that schedule and documents the full ISA review, including progress and how satisfied the individual is with their supports.

Changes between reviews. Bring changes in the individual’s goals or support needs to the team as soon as they come up. You can start the review and change process at any time. For a significant change in supports, update the formal ISA and get written approval before the change takes effect. That includes the individual, their guardian if applicable, the QDDP, and the agencies providing services, consistent with who approved the original ISA.

Document the change. Use the DDSD ISA Review/Change Form or an equivalent record. Include the individual’s name, the ISA term, the effective date, what is changing, who will provide support, how progress will be recorded and reviewed, and the required approvals. Use STAR to draft the proposed card and gather input. Once the formal change is approved, update the STAR card to match.

Vermont Critical Incident Reporting

For caregivers and employers of record supporting someone in a Vermont DDSD program. Vermont DAIL directs DDSD programs to report critical incidents through its online Critical Incident Reporting (CIR) system.

  1. Follow your team’s immediate safety and notification procedures. Notify the employer of record or designated contact according to your team’s reporting plan.
  2. Check the current Vermont CIR policy for the incident type, required notifications, and deadlines. For self- or family-managed services, coordinate with your case manager or designated reporting contact so the authorized person can submit through the state system.
  3. Gather the facts for the report, including when and where it happened, what happened, actions taken, and who was notified. Use your team’s approved secure process for sharing these details.

Use the Vermont DAIL portal for the official submission. STAR provides these resource links.

  • Open Vermont DAIL’s CIR portal and reporting instructions
  • Open Vermont DDSD Critical Incident Reporting Guidelines
  • Find the Critical Incident Reporting Form on Vermont’s DDSD forms page (reference copy).
