Caregiver tools

Caregiver Check-in

Record how the individual’s day went. From Home, open Check-in, confirm the date, and answer the questions that apply. Review and submit when you’re done.

Open Caregiver Check-in
Saved Drafts

Open an unfinished check-in saved on this device. Continue where you left off or delete a draft you no longer need.

Open Saved Drafts
Reports

Choose a date range to review check-in summaries, trends, and available ISA progress reports.

Open Reports
ISA Outcomes

Open an outcome to see the individual’s goal and how progress is measured. Admins can manage outcomes and link matching check-in answers to measures.

Open ISA Outcomes
Focus of the Week

Choose one area for the team to work on, describe the plan, and save it. Revisit it to reflect and plan next steps.

Open Focus of the Week
Team Updates

Admins can record medication or routine changes, learning goals, and other important updates for the team.

Open Team Updates
Review My STAR Observations

Admins can review submissions from Something I Did and decide whether an observation belongs with an ISA outcome.

Open Review My STAR Observations
Customize Caregiver Check-in

Admins can select a caregiver, assign relevant questions, and review links to ISA measures. Save when finished.

Open Customize Caregiver Check-in

Photos and monthly review

Add a Moment

Choose the individual, take or upload a photo, and add a short activity label, category, and caregiver observation. Save it for the individual to review.

Open Add a Moment
My Moments to Review

Look at one photo at a time. Share how it felt and whether you want to do it again. Add your own words if you’d like. Choose Not now to come back later. A photo can also appear after the mood check-in.

Open My Moments to Review
My Month

Choose an individual and a month to look back at their experiences. Open a photo to review their response and the support they used. Caregiver observations are shown separately on completed moments.

Open My Month

My STAR

Help Now and Something Hurts

Tap either tile in the first row to post to your test group chat right away. Add details if you want. In Something Hurts, choose Front or Back, tap a body part, and pick a symptom or save the body part alone. Get someone nearby if you need help right away.

Open Help Now and Something Hurts
How I Feel and My Day

Choose a face to share how you feel, or open My Day to share how an activity went. Tap Done to save your answer or Not now to skip.

Open How I Feel and My Day
Something I Did

Share something you did, tried, or learned. Add your own words and say if someone helped you. Tap Done to send it for team review.

Open Something I Did
Would You Rather?

Follow the picture choices to explore what you might like to do. Choose an activity, ask for something else, or stop whenever you want.

Open Would You Rather?
Talk to My Team

Open this tile to go to Group Chat or open picture cards.

Open Talk to My Team
Picture Communication

Open the picture cards and choose a picture to help communicate what you want to say.

Open Picture Communication
Read Aloud and Mic Setup

Turn on Read Aloud to hear prompts and supported choices. Use Mic Setup with a helper to try speaking into a supported text box. You can type in those boxes too.

Open Read Aloud and Mic Setup

Team tools and settings

Home

Use Quick Actions to open common tools. An unread Help Now or Something Hurts message from another team member appears at the top and links to Group Chat.

Open Home
Group Chat

Open Chat, type a message, and tap Send. The chat belongs to your active group. Opening it marks incoming messages as read.

Open Group Chat
Calendar

Open Calendar to see upcoming events for the group. Select an event to view details or use the add option to schedule something.

Open Calendar
Docs

Open Docs and choose a category to find care documents, ISA or IEP records, meeting minutes, or uploaded files.

Open Docs
Emergency Sheet

Open Emergency to find the individual’s key medical and emergency information. Admins manage changes to the sheet.

Open Emergency Sheet
Profile and Group Settings

Open Profile to see your account and group. Use Group Settings to manage your team if your role allows it.

Open Profile and Group Settings
